The drive wheel — how it grabs the belt

Final design, locked by measurement: the rim's flange walls are ground off (a clean cut — the hub's five 4.1 mm fins arch to the tunnel floor's underside and never touch the flanges), and the printed wheel sits on the untouched factory floor: Ø149 × 20.5. Ten teeth, rib 18×15, tooth marks every 56.2 mm on the rib (36°). The no-cut twin (Rev 009, 11 teeth on the factory rim) remains available and upgradeable.
