Section through the M16×195 pivot bolt (threaded 45 mm on one end only — a real headed bolt, not a double-end stud). z = 0 is the pod centreline; the bushing/plate stack is mirror-symmetric, the bolt itself is not (head one end, thread the other). Hover any part to highlight it in both views. Parts that share the same z range never touch — they sit at different radii, nested one inside the other.

How to read the overlaps: the trailing boss (11.35–36.35) and the trailing plate (30–36.35) overlap in z because the tube passes through the plate's Ø30.8 hole — plate at radius 16–22, tube at 11–15.5. Same for the bushing bodies inside the bosses (radius 8–11 inside 11–15.5), and the axle inside everything. The only parts that meet end-to-end at the same radius are the thrust chain: spacer → washer → flange → boss → washer → boss → flange → washer → sleeve → carrier — which is why the nylock can snug the whole stack. The bolt itself is asymmetric: a plain 10 mm head at −90…−80, then 195 mm of shank/thread running all the way to +115 — the nut (83…99) sits well inside that span, 16 mm of bare thread poking past it, and the smooth section (−80…+70) clears the farthest bushing edge (+65.85) by 4 mm before the thread starts.
