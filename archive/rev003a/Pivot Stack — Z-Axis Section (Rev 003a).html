How to read the overlaps: the trailing boss (11.35–36.35) and the trailing plate (30–36.35) overlap in z because the tube passes through the plate's Ø31.8 hole — plate at radius 16–22, tube at 11–16. Same for the bushing bodies inside the bosses (radius 8–11 inside 11–16), and the axle inside everything. The only parts that meet end-to-end at the same radius are the thrust chain: spacer → washer → flange → boss → washer → boss → flange → washer → sleeve → carrier — which is why the nylock can snug the whole stack. The 5 mm of empty bore inside each boss (the bushing is 20 in a 25 tube) shows as a gap in the Ø16–22 row.
